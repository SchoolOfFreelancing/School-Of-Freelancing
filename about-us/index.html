About School Of Freelancing

Turning beginners into freelancers, solving business challenges.

What is School Of Freelancing?

School of Freelancing provides instructor-led Linux and AI freelancing training, hands-on technical education, and professional cloud and server support for freelancers, businesses, developers and IT teams worldwide.

School Of Freelancing at a Glance

  • Businesses and IT teams that need hands-on Linux, cloud, VoIP/SMS or AI infrastructure support
