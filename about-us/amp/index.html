Mission & Vision

Why We're Obsessed With Linux

Linux powers most servers, cloud platforms, and DevOps pipelines. We put that power and its income in your hands.

Our Mission

To democratize Linux and AI skills worldwide, turning technical training into freelance income and robust business infrastructure solutions.

Our Vision

To power the global freelance economy through Linux and AI literacy while eliminating business revenue loss from server downtime.

Instructor Background

As Captain, Karim designs and personally teaches every training in the School Of Freelancing curriculum, translating real client-support work into practical, job-ready training rather than theory alone.

Training Methodology

Courses follow a learn-by-doing model: live sessions on real servers and APIs, project-based assignments mirroring actual freelance gigs, and direct mentorship until a student lands paid work. Content is continuously updated based on live client-support cases handled by the School.

Client-Support Experience

Beyond training, Karim and the School Of Freelancing team directly support businesses with Linux server management, cloud migration, VoIP/SMS infrastructure, and AI automation — see Client Support for active service areas.

Organization History

Founded in 2015, School Of Freelancing has grown from individual mentorship into a full training and support organization, expanding its curriculum to cover FusionPBX, Telnyx, Twilio, OpenAI, and Claude developer training alongside its original Linux focus.
