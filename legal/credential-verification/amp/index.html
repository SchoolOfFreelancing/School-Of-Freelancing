Credential Verification Support

Effective date: September 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 13, 2026 · School Of Freelancing (schooloffreelancing.com)

Quick Answer

Credential Verification Support lets an employer, client or partner platform confirm that a named student completed training with School Of Freelancing. It is free ($0) if training was completed within the past year and $20 USD (non-refundable) per completed verification after that. We confirm only what the student has authorized, and standard requests are completed within 2–3 business days. To request it, use the request form, our contact page, or email office@schooloffreelancing.com.

1. What Credential Verification Support Is

Credential Verification Support is the service through which School Of Freelancing confirms directly to a requesting organization that a named student completed one of our freelancing training programs. Prospective employers, clients and partner platforms use it to check a candidate's training record with the school itself.

2. What We Confirm and What We Don't

We confirm training completion status with School Of Freelancing, limited to what the student has authorized for that request.

We can confirm (if authorized by the student) We do not provide
Enrollment and successful completion of training A full transcript, performance review, or reference letter
Training dates and completion status Training content beyond what the student has authorized
— Personal contact details, payment history, or unrelated training records

3. Eligibility & Fees

Time since training completion Fee
Within 1 year of completing training Free ($0)
More than 1 year after completing training $20 USD, non-refundable, per completed verification

Students who completed training within the past year receive Credential Verification Support at no cost. Once more than one year has passed since a student's training completion date, a $20 non-refundable fee applies for us to process and complete a verification request.

4. How an Organization Requests Verification

  1. Complete the request form below, or contact us through our contact page or at office@schooloffreelancing.com, stating that you are requesting Credential Verification Support.
  2. Provide the student's full name and any identifying details the student gave you (for example, an enrollment ID or approximate training dates) so we can locate the correct record.
  3. If the request falls outside the free 1-year window, arrange payment of the $20 verification fee before we begin processing.
  4. We confirm training completion status directly with your organization, limited to the information the student has authorized us to disclose.

5. The Student's Role

Students should tell us which organization will be contacting us and what information they are comfortable with us confirming, for example dates of training or completion status. We only share what the student has indicated should be provided to the requesting organization.

6. What Information We Share

We share only the information the student has authorized for that specific request, typically limited to confirming enrollment and successful completion of training with School Of Freelancing. We do not disclose a student's personal contact details, payment history, or unrelated training records to a requesting organization without the student's direction. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information more broadly.

7. Fees Are Non-Refundable

The $20 verification fee is non-refundable once a Credential Verification Support request has been submitted and processed, regardless of the outcome of the organization's own hiring or engagement decision.

8. Turnaround Time

Standard verification requests are completed within 2–3 business days once required student authorization and any applicable $20 USD fee are received. Older or archived records may take up to 5 business days.
