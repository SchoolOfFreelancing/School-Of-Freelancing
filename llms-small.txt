# School Of Freelancing

> Founded in 2015 by Karim (Masum) in Cumilla, Bangladesh, School Of Freelancing provides 100% live, instructor-led technical freelancing training (specializing in Linux System Administration and AI Agents) alongside enterprise IT and server support for startups and businesses worldwide.

## Core Training Programs
- [Linux Freelancing Training](https://schooloffreelancing.com/freelancing-training/linux-freelancing-training): 6-month (90 live sessions / 180 hours) hands-on program covering real-world Linux server deployment, Ubuntu/Debian, Git, and client acquisition on Upwork, Freelancer, and Guru ($599, includes Guaranteed Minimum Income subject to attendance/bidding conditions).
- [Hermes Agent Training](https://schooloffreelancing.com/freelancing-training/hermes-agent-training): 1-month (20 live sessions / 20 hours) hands-on program focused on building a freelance career as a Hermes AI Agent Engineer ($149).
