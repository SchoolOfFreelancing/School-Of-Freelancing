## What Is FusionPBX VoIP Support?

FusionPBX VoIP Support is School of Freelancing's done-for-you service for
FusionPBX, the open-source VoIP/PBX platform built on FreeSWITCH. An experienced
engineer handles installation, configuration, and troubleshooting — from basic
setup through advanced feature configuration — for businesses and individuals at
any skill level, delivered remotely, worldwide.
