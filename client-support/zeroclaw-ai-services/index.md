## What Is ZeroClaw Support?

ZeroClaw Support is School of Freelancing's done-for-you service for ZeroClaw, a
personal AI assistant agent runtime that connects to LLM providers including
Anthropic, OpenAI, Ollama, and around 20 others. We handle the setup,
configuration, and troubleshooting of your ZeroClaw installation and its
provider integrations, so businesses and individuals can run it without hiring a
full-time AI engineer.
