6. Technical Definitions & Industry Terms

Online Instructor-Led Training (ILT)
An educational format where a live instructor directs teaching in real-time over video and voice, enabling continuous back-and-forth guidance, live terminal pairing, and immediate answer feedback.
Guaranteed Minimum Income (GMI)*
An institutional career program that assures a baseline financial earnings floor for qualified graduates who execute all training tasks, lab assessments, and client application requirements.
Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS)
Software published under licenses permitting anyone to inspect, modify, and enhance the code, creating zero licensing friction for enterprise client deployments.
Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP)
A group of technologies for delivering voice communications and multimedia sessions over IP networks, powered by platforms like Asterisk, FreeSWITCH, and FusionPBX.
DevOps and Continuous Integration / Deployment (CI/CD)
A modern engineering methodology uniting system operations and development through automated test runners, Docker container packaging, and reliable cloud deployments.
