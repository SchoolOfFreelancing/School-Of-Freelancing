# Resource Center | School of Freelancing

School of Freelancing maintains comprehensive resources to support both training
participants and professional service clients. Whether you're preparing to
enroll in hands-on Linux freelancing training, considering our professional
Linux infrastructure and AI services, or exploring your freelancing career
options, our resources provide transparent information about program structure,
expectations, and real student and client outcomes.

Our FAQ section covers essential topics: training program structure and
scheduling, payment methods and financial commitments, technical prerequisites
and system requirements, credential verification and student portfolio support,
career advancement strategies for freelancers, and frequently asked questions
about our professional service offerings. The Testimonials section features
verified reviews from students who have completed our intensive training
programs and businesses that have engaged our expert Linux administration, AI
infrastructure, SMS gateway, VoIP, and trading bot deployment services. All
testimonials reflect genuine participant experiences and measurable outcomes
across diverse technical domains.
