Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hermes Agent Training, and who is it for?

A live, instructor-led 4-week course (20 sessions, 20 hours, $149 one-time) where developers, freelancers, and agency owners learn to deploy, harden, and automate production Hermes AI agents on Linux servers and win global freelance clients. It is 100% live with no pre-recorded videos, taught by Karim (Masum), at Intermediate to Advanced level.

What prerequisites and accounts do I need before joining?

You need a DigitalOcean VPS account (Ubuntu Linux server), a domain or subdomain for SSL configuration, verified profiles on Upwork, Freelancer, or Guru, and a reliable internet connection for live hands-on labs. See the Training Rules.

How does the 4-week curriculum prepare me for freelance AI agent projects?

Across 20 live sessions, you deploy Hermes Agent with Nginx/SSL, connect Ollama and cloud LLMs, build Telegram and Discord bots, publish a GitHub portfolio repository, and complete live Upwork proposal and client onboarding exercises. Clients and earnings are not guaranteed. Ask your mentor on Telegram or read the Refund Policy before enrolling.
