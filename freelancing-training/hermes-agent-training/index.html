Hermes Agent Freelancing Training

Overview

Learn production deployment of Hermes Agent and package your technical setup into freelance services. Build practical skills for Linux server deployment, AI integrations, troubleshooting, client delivery, and client acquisition through Upwork, Freelancer, Guru, LinkedIn, and YouTube.

About Training

AspectDetail
Duration:20 hands-on training sessions over one month.
Price:$149 (one-time investment).
Format:Live online, instructor-led, hands-on training with no pre-recorded videos.
Covers:Deploy Hermes Agent in a production environment, integrate AI services, troubleshoot deployments, package technical services, and prepare for freelance client work.
Best for:Developers, freelancers, and agency owners who want practical Hermes Agent deployment and AI infrastructure skills for global client work.
Income support:Freelance marketplace application guidance is included. Results depend on attendance, skills, applications, marketplace opportunities, and client demand; marketplace bids or connects may be required.

Participation Requirements

Training Prerequisites

  • DigitalOcean account, a domain or subdomain, and a freelance marketplace profile.
  • Commitment: Patience, concentration, and active participation during all training sessions.

Connectivity

  • A reliable device for training and client communication.
  • Reliable internet access for uninterrupted live training sessions.

Training Rules

  • Training Rules apply to all School of Freelancing trainees to keep training fair, focused, and productive.
