Project-based training for Autonomous AI Agent Deployment Specialists.

Overview

You'll become a production-ready OpenClaw specialist with a live, working deployment of a 24/7 autonomous AI agent proving real hands-on skills. Earn via freelance gigs, direct client contracts, or supporting businesses that want an always-on digital assistant of their own.

Why Join This Training?

Autonomous AI agents are one of the fastest-moving categories on Upwork, Guru, and Freelancer right now, and clients are actively searching for someone who can stand up a 24/7 digital assistant they actually own — not a subscription to someone else's platform. Most freelancers can't fill that request because they've never taken OpenClaw past a local demo. This 5-day sprint changes that: you leave with a production deployment, 3 marketplace profiles, and 5 service videos as instant proof of work, plus the job search and client delivery skills to convert that portfolio into paid work fast.

You'll leave with:

1

Hands-on experience deploying and configuring a live, production-grade OpenClaw agent

2

Marketplace portfolio proving real deployment, troubleshooting, and client-support skills

3

Stronger habits for configuring, maintaining, and troubleshooting autonomous AI agents under real workloads
