Frequently Asked Questions

What is School of Freelancing and what do you do?

School of Freelancing provides Linux and AI-related instructor-led training for freelancers, and reliable technical client support for businesses.

What technologies does School of Freelancing teach and support?

We teach and support Linux system administration, cloud infrastructure, AI agent deployment, VoIP, and bulk SMS API integration, all using free and open-source software (FOSS).

What is Guaranteed Minimum Income (GMI)* into any training?

Our Guaranteed Minimum Income (GMI)* applies under two conditions: maintain zero-leave attendance throughout the training, and purchase sufficient bids/connects on freelance marketplaces to search, apply, and get hired for jobs.
